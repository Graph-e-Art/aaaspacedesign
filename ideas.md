# AA Space Design — Design Direction

## Ground truth

Use supplied Figma interior-design landing page as layout reference. Preserve its visual rhythm: generous white space, large Inter headings, image-led editorial sections, dark CTA blocks, thin rules, three-column service cards, process timeline, testimonials, newsletter, and multi-column footer.

## Brand adaptation

- Public brand: AA Space Design
- Domain: aaspacedesign.com
- Core palette: Brand Red `#BB0305`, Walnut Brown `#8A5A44`, Charcoal `#1C1C1C`, Warm White `#FAF8F5`, Sand `#E8DED4`
- Brand essence: warm architectural clarity
- Brand voice: professional, practical, confident, approachable
- Services: commercial interiors, space planning, office partitioning, civil and structural work, electrical and plumbing, HVAC, IT infrastructure, carpentry and woodwork, glass installation, full turnkey projects

## Design dimensions

- Movement: warm minimal architecture; editorial composition rather than generic template UI.
- Principles: clarity, measured spacing, real imagery, strong hierarchy, practical conversion.
- Layout: responsive grid replacing Figma absolute coordinates on smaller screens.
- Signature elements: short red rule, walnut metadata, charcoal blocks, split image/text modules, outlined architectural frame.
- Interaction: smooth scroll, hover lift, accordion service rows, accessible buttons and focus states.
- Animation: restrained fade/slide reveal; disabled under reduced-motion preference.
- Typography: Inter; large semibold headings, readable body copy, uppercase eyebrow labels.
- Logo: supplied AA Space Design logo at `/assets/aa-space-design-logo.png`; do not invent claims.
- Imagery: supplied local Figma imagery; use only assets copied into `public/assets`.
