Privacy policy

Short notice for website enquiries

AA Associate uses the details you submit to review and respond to your project enquiry.

Information collected

The enquiry form asks for your name, email, phone or WhatsApp number, project type, city and message. Budget and preferred call date or time are optional.

How information is used

Submitted details are saved with the enquiry so AA Associate can review your requirements and follow up. The system may also send an enquiry notification to the business and a confirmation to the email address you provide.

Sharing and care

Do not include passwords, payment details or other sensitive information in the enquiry form. Contact details are not displayed in public project listings.

Questions

For a question about an enquiry or this notice, contact AA Associate.
