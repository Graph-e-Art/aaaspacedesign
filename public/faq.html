Questions, answered.

A short guide to starting an interior or furniture project with AA Associate.

What work can I discuss with AA Associate?

Custom sofas and furniture, space planning, interior work, renovation and related project coordination. The exact scope is agreed for each enquiry.

Do you work on homes and commercial spaces?

Yes. Enquiries can cover residential, office and commercial spaces. Share the type of space and your priorities so the team can understand the brief.

Where do you serve?

AA Associate lists Karachi, Islamabad, Lahore and projects across Pakistan. Availability for a specific location can be confirmed when you enquire.

How do I get started?

Send your project type, city, requirements and preferred timing through the enquiry form. The team can then discuss the next step and scope.

How is a project estimate prepared?

It depends on the space, selected work and project requirements. Share the details first so the scope can be discussed before an estimate is prepared.
