## Visual language

Quiet, furniture-first interior design: editorial serif display headings, restrained sans-serif body copy, square-edged buttons, generous whitespace, and authentic project photography. Keep the existing navigation, logo, enquiry flow, and section order intact when extending this page.

## Typography

- Body and navigation: Inter, 400–700.
- Display headings in the service card and dialog: Georgia / Times New Roman serif stack.
